# Inner-loop bandwidth vs sway (issue 173)

Tables generated by `scripts/inner_loop_bandwidth.py` (~7 min); the conclusion is by hand and a re-run overwrites it.

Loop: `u = (p + d s) e_pos` (JTC PI, feed-forward excluded, clamp ignored) -> delay 65 ms (C3 60 ms + half a 100 Hz ZOH sample) -> PT1 `tau_v` -> `tau' = k (u_f - dq)` -> body. Body linearised at the hanging equilibrium, other planned axes held; **locked** = pendulum rigid (M_ii), **free** = pendulum as the OCP models it (rows [i, tip, tilt] of M, dh/dq, dh/ddq). Units rad/s (w), kg m^2 or kg (M). `sway` = lowest pendulum mode, actuated locked. `BW` = -3 dB of `q_ref -> q` without feed-forward. GM `inf` = no -180 crossing.

## Conclusion

1. **Not transparent.** At shipped gains BW / w_sway is 0.02-0.03 slew, 0.5-1.0 boom, 0.35-0.85 arm, 1.9-2.4 rotator, 4.2-6.9 telescope. Only the telescope approaches 5x, and it loses that with 800 kg at some poses.
2. **Worse than slow: not robust.** With the free pendulum and C3's lightly damped force-state resonance sqrt(k/M_ii), the shipped gains fail PM >= 45 or are unstable at 26/96 (boom) and 40/96 (arm) unloaded poses, and at 82/96, 90/96, 51/96 and 22/96 (boom, arm, telescope, slew) with 800 kg. What binds is the gain margin, not the dead time. Unloaded on boom and arm, the unstable pole *is the sway mode* (2.5-2.9 rad/s). It grows slowly: e-fold 20-100 s against the model's passive damping of 15/20 N m s/rad, and 2-3x that damping restabilises it. Heavy and locked, it is C3's force-state resonance sqrt(k/M_ii). (This fits q2 oscillating on hardware under `controller:=pid`.)
3. **It cannot be made transparent.** The best robust (p, d) (PM >= 45, GM >= 6 dB over the grid) gives a worst-pose BW of 0.05/0.02 slew, 0.36/0.17 boom, 0.27/0.11 arm, 21/0.55 telescope and 10 rad/s rotator (0 / 800 kg). Only the rotator (4.3x sway) and the unloaded telescope (8x) come near 5x. On slew, boom and arm the best `d` is 0: the e_vel term feeds the resonance.
4. The PI therefore sits at or below the sway frequency on the axes that make sway. It is part of the plant the MPC drives, not a servo underneath it. The OCP should model it (issue 173, "sensitive" branch), or the PI should shrink to an offset corrector.
5. Caveats: C3 damping `d` is fitted near the median M_ii and extrapolated to the 10-40x heavier poses. The payload is a point mass (no rotator inertia). The grid is the control-safe box, not a load chart: 800 kg at full reach is included. Gazebo has not cross-checked this. Coulomb friction on tip/tilt (10/5 N m in the Gazebo URDF) is not in the linear model and adds amplitude-dependent damping. Many box poses are not collision-free (ground, self).
